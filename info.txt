STRATEGY SUMMARY (seller's perspective):
- We NEVER trade spot/futures. Only option PREMIUM charts.
- HTF (75-min): bearish trap = curr LOW < prev LOW → bears short → HIGH > prev HIGH
  → bears TRAPPED. Zone = Ref Bar LOW (top) to Next Bar LOW (bottom).
- LTF (5-min): same pattern INSIDE the HTF zone. Select lowest Zone Low per HTF zone.
- ENTRY: price returns to LTF Ref Bar LOW (5-min sellers' SL hit) → BUY premium.
- TARGET (T1): HTF Ref Bar HIGH (75-min sellers' SL level).
- PHASE 3 SL: counterpart option fires its OWN LTF entry (inside ITS own HTF zone)
  → exit at active side's current bar CLOSE.
  SL only valid if counterpart entry price > previous counterpart entry price
  (counterpart gaining upward momentum = market genuinely reversing).
